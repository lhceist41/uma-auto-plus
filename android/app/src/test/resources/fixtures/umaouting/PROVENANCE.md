# Uma Outing popup fixture provenance

Rows 240 to 470 (full width) of full-resolution (1080x1920) screencaps from MuMu, taken on 2026-11-13 during the
Uma Outing campaign. Ground truth for `LabelUmaOutingBanner` (`components/label/uma_outing_banner.png`, cropped from
the first frame at x 190 to 430, y 310 to 400).

| Fixture | Shows |
|---|---|
| login_bonus.png | The "Let's Go! Uma Outing!" banner of the Outing Login Bonus popup shown over Home after the game-day reset. Its only control is a "TAP" bar (rows 1544 to 1640 of the frame). |
| gauge_max.png | The same banner on the "Outing Gauge MAX! Ready to go!" popup shown over the Daily Races runner list after a multi-race. Its control is a green Next. |

## Source and colour

The screencaps keep the device colours as they are, so these PNGs need no red/blue swap. Nothing in the game UI was
altered. The crop keeps only the banner and the blurred screen behind it: no trainer name, ID or other identifying
data is present.
