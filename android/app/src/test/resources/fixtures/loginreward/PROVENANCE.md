# Login reward fixture provenance

Rows 1660 to 1920 (full width) of full-resolution (1080x1920) screencaps from MuMu, taken on 2026-11-27 right after
the game's daily reset (15:00 UTC). Ground truth for `ButtonSkip` (`components/button/skip.png`) on the login reward
screens, which wait for a tap on their round ">>" button at the bottom right.

| Fixture | Shows |
|---|---|
| anniversary_countdown.png | The bottom of the 2nd Anniversary Countdown reward screen ("Obtained Carats" x150 above this crop) with its ">>" button. |
| login_bonus.png | The bottom of the LOGIN BONUS screen: the last row of the day's rewards and the ">>" button. |

## Source and colour

The screencaps keep the device colours as they are, so these PNGs need no red/blue swap. Nothing in the game UI was
altered. The crop keeps only the bottom strip (reward icons, the blurred background and the button): no trainer name,
rank, ID or other identifying data is present.
