# Goal counter fixtures

Crops of the career goal box, no names, IDs or club.

- `trophy_counter_1080x1920.png`: a 720x140 crop (x 0-720, y 140-280) of a MuMu 1080x1920 Race List screen with the goal "In G1, place within the top 5 1 time(s)" and the "1 time(s) left" counter; the calendar's "turn(s) left" is inside the crop.
- `fan_counter_1080x1920.png`: a 780x130 crop (x 0-780, y 100-230) of a MuMu 1080x1920 Grand Concert Training screen with the goal "Earn 3000 fans" and the "1,714 fan(s) to go" counter.
- `trophy_counter_scaled_094.png`, `fan_counter_scaled_094.png`, `*_scaled_092.png`, `*_scaled_088.png`: SYNTHETIC. The two crops above resized to 0.94, 0.92 and 0.88 (OpenCV INTER_AREA), standing in for tall 1080-wide screens that draw the counters smaller (0.94 measured on 1080x2340; tall phones drew other text at 0.96, 0.92 and 0.88). No real tall-screen frame with a goal counter was available.
- `goal_box_no_counter_1080x2316.png`: a 720x180 crop (x 0-720, y 190-370) of a 1080x2316 phone career screen whose goal "Run in Junior Make Debut" has no counter; the calendar's "turn(s) left" is inside the crop.
