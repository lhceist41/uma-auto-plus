Crops of 1080x1920 `adb screencap` frames (true RGB) from MuMu, taken on 2026-11-25 just before a queue was started on
each screen. They pin the Home-tab templates in `MenuScreenHomeTabFixtureTest`.

| Fixture | Source | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| event_missions_bottom_bar.png | Story Event "Event Missions" page, y 1770 to 1905 | The bottom bar with the Home tab unselected. |
| veteran_roster_bottom_bar.png | Veteran Roster, y 1770 to 1905 | The bottom bar with Enhance selected and the Home tab unselected. |
| missions_bottom_bar.png | Missions (Daily), y 1770 to 1905 | The bottom bar with the Home tab selected. |

The crops hold only the bottom bar: no trainer name, ID, rank, roster or friend code.
